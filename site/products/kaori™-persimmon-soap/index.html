How do i know if i have nonenal?

Nonenal is a natural compound that forms when skin oils oxidize, creating a greasy or musty smell. This can become more noticeable in your 50s and beyond as a normal part of aging. The catch: you may not smell it yourself. Your nose adapts to your own scent, while others may still notice it.

Why buy from KAORI?

Why does that matter? Persimmon extract is the ingredient; tannins are compounds within it. A high extract percentage doesn’t necessarily mean high tannin levels.
